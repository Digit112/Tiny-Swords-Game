# Introduction

Singleplayer, lightweight RTS on a generated square grid with resource and unit
management, includes city-building elements.

## Genre & Core Mechanics

- Real-Time Strategy
- Singleplayer
- City-Builder (Buildings Generate Units and Resources)
- Unit Management (Control of individuals and groups)
- Resource Harvesting and Economy
- Combat
- Sailing
- Procedurally Generated

## Why Fun ?

1. Intellectual Problem Solving
2. Competition
3. Power

# Gameplay Experience

Worlds are randomly generated on a square grid. Kingdoms spawn with a small party of units and resources to begin. The player may place buildings and command units, navigating the world with a god's eye view.

The map contains finite wood and gold, but these resources are fully recycleable - destroying a kingdom will cause the conquerer to claim all the resources it ever mined.

## Core Loop

The player continuously analyzes the coditions of the map while performing the following actions:

- Command Units to Patrol, Guard, Attack, and Mine
- Order Building Construction and Destruction
- Order Units to be Training

Units are selected by click or rectangular select and commanded using the mouse.

## Objectives & Progression

The objective of a game is always to overtake the other kingdoms.
The player progresses by harvesting resources and expanding production to amass power.
